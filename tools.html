Tools

Convert GTFS feeds into Linked Connections and run route planning algorithms on the client.

gtfs2lc

Convert GTFS Schedule feeds into connections ordered by departure time. Export RDF and JSON-LD for Linked Connections 1.0, or generate Apache Arrow tables and a JSON-LD manifest for Linked Connections 2.0-alpha, including stops, trips, routes, transfers, and paged connections.

iRail planner

A JavaScript route planning library for browsers and Node.js, built specifically for Belgium and adaptable to your own data and routing needs. It reads Linked Connections 2.0-alpha Arrow data and calculates routes locally.

  • Journey alternatives across multiple networks, with walking transfers.
  • Isochrones showing which stops can be reached within a time limit.
  • Station or coordinate-based routing, including walking to and from stops.

The current alpha uses scheduled timetables. Its playground includes a route planner, isochrone maps, and library documentation, and lets you load your own timetable.
